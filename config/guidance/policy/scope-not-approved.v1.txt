PROJECT-GUIDANCE SCOPE NOT APPROVED — v1
PROVENANCE CLASS: DISPATCHER_AUTHORED. This is the canonical failure text the
dispatcher returns to Sol when a task's allowed_paths intersect a repository scope
that has a local CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md but NO approved curated projection. It is never
projected to a worker, because no worker is dispatched in this case.

TYPED ERROR: ProjectGuidanceNotApproved

This task's allowed_paths intersect the scope "{scope_prefix}" (manifest logical id
"{logical_id}"). That scope has project instruction files in the repository, but its
curated project-guidance projection has NOT been reviewed and approved.

The dispatch is refused and no worker was started.

There is deliberately NO fallback to root-only guidance. Root guidance does not carry
that subproject's domain invariants — its source-of-truth modules, its
do-not-reintroduce decisions, its gating test suites, its telephony, retrieval and
session rules — and dispatching a worker without them would silently authorise work
against guidance that was never reviewed for it.

To proceed, approve a curated projection for "{scope_prefix}" and add it to
config/approved-guidance.json with classification CURATED_SUBPROJECT, or narrow the
task's allowed_paths so they no longer intersect that scope.

Scopes currently in this state: "BSL Agent/", "HattonHills/", "Sofia Agent/".
